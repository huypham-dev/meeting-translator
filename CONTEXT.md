# Meeting Translator

A Chrome extension that captures meeting audio and shows live English to Vietnamese captions, and keeps notes of what was said.

## Language

**Caption**:
The live on-screen line pair (English source, Vietnamese translation) shown while someone speaks.
_Avoid_: Subtitle

**Meeting**:
One translation session, from a Start to its Stop; the unit that notes are saved under.
_Avoid_: Session, call, recording

**Transcript**:
The full, timestamped record of finalized spoken sentences in a Meeting, in the language that was heard (no translation).
_Avoid_: Log, history

**Summary**:
An AI-written digest of a Meeting's Transcript (key points, decisions, action items), in the same language as the Transcript, generated on demand.
_Avoid_: Recap, minutes

**Notes**:
A saved Meeting's Transcript plus its Summary, if one has been generated; exportable as a Markdown file.
_Avoid_: Minutes, record
